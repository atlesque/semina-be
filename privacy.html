Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

In short
  • You can play without anything about you being stored on our server.
  • Your best scores and leaderboard name stay in your own browser.
  • Only when you save a score to the global leaderboard do we store your name and score, together with your IP address and a browser fingerprint, so we can keep bots and cheaters off the board.
  • We delete the IP address and fingerprint after 90 days. No cookies, ads, analytics or selling of data.

Who is responsible

Pini Arcade (semina.be) is run by Atlesque Media VOF, based in Belgium, which is the data controller for the personal data described here. For any privacy question or request, email privacy@semina.be.

What we process and why

Playing the game

The game runs in your browser. It keeps your records, your local leaderboard and the last name you entered in your browser's local storage (pini.records.v1, pini.leaderboard.v1, pini.player.v1). This data never leaves your device unless you save a score, and you can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. We do not use cookies.

Saving a score to the global leaderboard

Saving is optional. When you press Save score, we store:

DataPurposeKept
The name you enter, your score, the game mode and the timeShowing the global leaderboard. This name is public, so don't use your real name if you'd rather not.Until the score is removed from the board
Your IP address, and what our host derives from it: country, approximate city and region, network provider (ASN), timezoneDetecting cheating, bots and abuse of the leaderboard90 days
Your browser's user agent and connection details (TLS and HTTP version, Cloudflare data center)Detecting cheating, bots and abuse90 days
A browser fingerprint: technical characteristics of your browser and device (how it draws a test image and plays a silent test sound, graphics card, installed fonts from a fixed list, screen size, language, timezone, number of processor cores, memory, touch support, and whether the browser is controlled by automation software), plus a code computed from themRecognising when many scores come from the same device, to catch cheating and bots90 days

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a fair leaderboard and protecting it from abuse (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We only collect the fingerprint and IP when you choose to save a score, we keep them for a limited time, and we use them for nothing else: not for advertising, profiling or tracking you across other websites. Reading the fingerprint from your browser at that moment is strictly necessary for the leaderboard service you asked for.

Only the site administrator can see the IP address and fingerprint. They may use it to remove scores that look automated or fraudulent.

Who else handles data

Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the site and the leaderboard database (stored in Western Europe) as our processor. Like any web host, it handles your IP address to deliver pages and to protect the site from attacks. Cloudflare is a US company certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Everything the game needs, including its fonts, is served from semina.be itself, so apart from our host no other company sees your visit. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you, and get a copy of it;
  • have it corrected or deleted, including removing your score from the leaderboard;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest, or ask us to restrict it.

Email privacy@semina.be and tell us your leaderboard name, roughly when you saved the score and, if you can, the score itself, so we can find it. We answer within one month.

If you think we handle your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données), dataprotectionauthority.be, or to the authority in the EU country where you live.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and the date at the top.
